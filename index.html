What is Bluer Network?

Bluer Network is a Legacy Console Edition multiplayer server. We are one of the oldest running LCE servers with our own authentication system and a laid-back community of players who love Minecraft: Legacy Console Edition.

How to Connect

1Join the Discord server via the button above
2Complete verification in #how-to-register
3Post your in-game username in #register-account
4Join at bluer-network.ddns.net and run /verify <code>

Security & Anti-Cheat

  • Authentication: Our custom auth system tracks you by UUID, IP, and name — your identity and inventory stay safe even when LCE changes your UUID.
  • Conduct Policy: No bigotry, No toxicity. Any slurs are a permanent ban.
  • Moderation: Our moderators keep the server safe and enjoyable for everyone. Report any issues and we'll handle it.
